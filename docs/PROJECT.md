# Project Definition


## Product Vision

A simple retail inventory and sales system.


The main problem solved:

Store owners need to know:

- What they have
- What they sold
- What they need to order


## Target Users


Owner:

- manages products
- checks inventory
- receives stock


Employee:

- creates orders
- searches products


## Current Scope


Included:

- Product catalog
- Product variants
- Stock
- Orders
- Inventory movements


Not included:

- Customers
- Accounting
- Loyalty
- Suppliers
- Payments
- Advanced reports
